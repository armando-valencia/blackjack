# M2 — Responsive Cards

Branch: `feature/mobile-responsive-cards`

## Scope

- Scale cards based on available space.
- Use controlled wrapping or overlap for larger hands.
- Keep rank and suit legible at the smallest supported size.
- Prevent hands from hiding actions below the viewport.

## Acceptance criteria

- Hands of at least six cards remain usable on a phone.
- Card content is readable without zooming.
- Dynamic card counts do not hide actions.
