# Accessibility Checklist

Run this checklist for responsive gameplay changes at `320x568`, `390x844`, and `1280x800` viewport sizes.

## Keyboard

- Tab through player selection, the rules summary, reset, and every available game action.
- Confirm every focused control has a visible focus indicator.
- Confirm gameplay works with Enter or Space and never traps focus behind an overlay.

## Screen reader

- Confirm dealer phase, game messages, action guidance, connection changes, and errors are announced.
- Confirm cards expose rank, suit, and hidden-hole-card labels.
- Confirm turn, status, result, score, and session statistics are understandable without color.

## Responsive layout

- Confirm there is no horizontal scrolling at either phone width.
- Confirm dealer cards, the human hand, action rail, and rules summary remain reachable on short screens.
- Confirm six-card hands wrap without hiding the action rail or clipping card content.

## Visual preferences

- Confirm text and controls remain readable against their surfaces.
- Confirm Hit, Stand, Deal, Play Again, and Reset have comfortable touch targets.
- Enable reduced motion and confirm gameplay remains complete without animation-dependent information.
